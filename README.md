🤖 Agentic AI — Learning & Hands-on Work Samples
Welcome to Agentic AI — a practical collection of Agentic AI concepts, experiments, implementations, and hands-on work samples designed to help you learn how AI agents are built, orchestrated, and applied to real-world problems.

This repository is intended to be a learning-by-doing playground for anyone exploring the rapidly evolving world of Agentic AI.

Rather than focusing only on theory, the goal is to build, experiment, compare approaches, and understand the building blocks behind intelligent AI agents.
